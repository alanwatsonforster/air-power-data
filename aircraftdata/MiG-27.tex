\subsection{Versions}

\subsubsection{MiG-23B}

The MiG-23B is the original strike version. Its NATO reporting name is Flogger-F.
It first flew in 1971.
Changes from the interceptor version include the AL-21F engine with 17,200 lbf of dry thrust and 24,700 lbf of afterburner thrust, the second-generation wing with greater area but no leading-edge flaps, additional armor, more robust landing gear, more fuel, and additional weapon stations on the rear fuselage.
It retains the GSh-23L cannon of the MiG-23.
The air-to-air radar and the original nose are replaced with  a laser tracker and a navigation radar in a low-profile nose that gives much better forward visibility.
A Siren-FSh jammer and a Siren-10M RWR are installed.%
\note{MiG-23B Technical Data}{
    See Wikipedia and Goebel.
}

Only about two dozen MiG-23B were built.
It entered service in the VVS-FA probably in 1971 or 1972, but was not exported as the AL-21F was restricted to Soviet use.%
\note{MiG-23B Service and Combat}{
    See Wikipedia and Goebel.
}

\subsection{Armament and Stores}

The MiG-23B is armed with the GSh-23L 23 mm gun with 200 rounds. At 3,500 rounds per minute this corresponds to 1.7 ammunition points.%
\note{Gun}{See Wikipedia on the MiG-27 and GSh-23.}

The MiG-23B usually carried a centerline FT.%
\note{FTs}{

}

UB-16-57 (\binarymultiply{16}{57~mm}) or UB-32-57 (\binarymultiply{32}{57~mm}) RPs were often carried on the forward-fuselage and wing-glove stations, and sometimes B-20 (\binarymultiply{20}{85~mm}) RPs, BL-13 (\binarymultiply{5}{122~mm}) RPs, or individual S-24 (240 mm) RKs.%
\note{RPs and RKs}{
    See Goebel and Wikipedia on the MiG-27.
}

From 1973, the MiG-23B can carry the Kh-23 (AS-7 Kerry) ASM on the wing-glove stations.%
\note{ASMs}{
    See Goebel, Wikipedia on the MiG-27, and Wikipedia on the Kh-23.
}

The MiG-23B can carry GSh-23L gun ponds on the wing-glove stations.%
\note{GPs}{
    See Goebel.
}

R-60 (AA-8 Aphid) or R-13S (AA-2 Atoll) IRMs can be carried on either the wing-glove stations or the forward fuselage stations, but not on both.
R-60s can be carried on dual-launcher racks.
