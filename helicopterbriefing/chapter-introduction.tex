\chapter{Introduction}

Helicopters have been part of warfare since the Korean War in the early 1950s, serving in roles that include transport, casualty evacuation, mobile artillery and anti-tank, and combat search and rescue.

This briefing document contains information on helicopters for the {\AirPow} game system. It first presents the helicopters and then gives helicopter-specific stores such as ATGMs.

The \href{https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cobra_and_Kiowa_DF-ST-86-10445.JPEG}{cover illustration} shows an AH-1 Cobra and OH-58 Kiowa in flight (US Army: Public Domain).
